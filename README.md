# Ascend · Road Damage Research

Project website for Group Ascend's IS4990 research proposal (Faculty of Information Technology, University of Moratuwa, 2026): detecting small and thin road damage in countries a detector has never seen, using RDD2022 and YOLO11.

Live site: https://amri2002.github.io/ascend-rdd/

Team: Chamikara J.R.H. (225019D), Amri B.M.M. (225007N), Imasha G.G.P. (225040H)
Supervisors: Prof. (Mrs) Thanuja Sandanayake, Dr. A.L.A. Romesh R. Thanuja

Dataset images: RDD2022 (Arya et al., 2024), CC BY-SA 4.0. Sample figures from oracl4/RoadDamageDetection and USC-InfoLab/rddc2020.
